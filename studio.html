Geisburg Studio is 5 minutes from Berlin-Wittenbergplatz and provides a cozy atmosphere for writing, mixing, and recording sessions.

Instruments include: Yamaha Upright Piano, Hammond Organ L500, Fully Miced Drum Kit, Moog Grandmother Analog Synthesizer, Dave Smith Mopho X4 Polyphonic Analog Synthesizer, Yamaha CP Reface Key-Module, Yamaha RX 14 & 17 Drum Machines, Yamaha SY77 FM Synthesizer, Fender Jazzmaster, Höfner Bass Guitar, Warwick Bass Guitar, multiple Acoustic Guitars and Bass Guitars, Glockenspiel, diverse Percussion, Yamaha HS8 Studio Monitors, and one Mono Auratone Studio Monitor. The whole setup is fully equipped with microphones and ready to go at any moment.

Reach out to info@geisburgrecords.com for studio sessions, mixing, mastering requests.
